A few things to know

Does it support Warcraft III patch 3.0, The Forsaken Kingdom?

Yes. Thae's Replay Explorer supports Warcraft III (Warcraft 3) patch 3.0, The Forsaken Kingdom. Browse .w3g replay files and compare heroes, ability levels, units, buildings, upgrades, items, APM, control groups and chat. Future replay-format changes may require an app update.

Can I analyze W3Champions replays?

Yes. Choose the folder containing your W3Champions replays to browse and analyze them alongside other Warcraft III matches. Filter by player, map, race matchup or team size, and click a player's full BattleTag to open their W3Champions profile.

How do I watch a replay in Warcraft III?

Double-click it in the library or choose Watch replay. Warcraft III and the matching map must be installed, and Windows must associate .w3g files with the game. The Watch replay button is available when the map is found on your PC. You do not need the game installed to browse replay analysis.

Where do my replays and settings go?

Your replay library, cached analysis and settings stay on your computer. The app does not upload your replays or collect telemetry. Folder changes are detected automatically; indexing a large library can take time on the first run.

What does the analysis measure?

Replay files record player commands, rather than a complete game simulation. Production and research entries reflect orders; hero levels are inferred minimums. Some damaged or unsupported replays cannot be parsed, and future game updates may require an app update.
